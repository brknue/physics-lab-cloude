What makes a euro coin valuable?
  • Country and year on the national side. Low-mintage issuers such as Monaco, Vatican City, San Marino and Andorra, and some commemorative 2 € coins, often sell above face value.
  • Greek coins from 2002 with an “S” inside the star come from the Finnish mint and are sought after.
  • Minting errors: a core that sits off-centre in the ring, a core and ring made from the same metal, an off-centre strike, doubled lettering, a clipped or missing-rim coin.
  • Most circulated euro coins are worth face value. Condition matters, so never clean a coin.
  • Photo tips: one denomination only, plain matte background, even daylight, coins not touching, camera straight above.
